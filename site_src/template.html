NFL fourth-down decision audit

Fourth Down Ledger

Every fourth down priced in win probability: go, punt or kick. Which coaches leave wins on the table, a decision chart a staff can print, and a calculator for any situation.

Decision chart

The call with the highest win probability for every yards-to-go and field position. Pale cells are toss-ups (under 0.5 points of win probability). Recomputed live in your browser by the same engine used for the audit.

What should they do?

Win probability left on the table

Sum over a team's fourth downs of (win probability of the best call minus that of the call made), measured before the snap, so outcomes never count. A total of 1.0 is one expected win. Bars show 95% bootstrap intervals.

How it works, and how well

Reliability map

Mean absolute gap between this project's win probability and nflverse's published model on the same 2025 plays (one-score games), by time left. Agreement is within about 2 points for most of the game and loosens in the final two minutes, where clock management dominates.

Models (all classical statistics, all fit on 2016-2024 regular and postseason play-by-play)

  • Expected points: least-squares regression of the next score in the half on a field-position spline, down, log distance and an end-of-half decay.
  • Win probability: ridge-penalised logistic regression on score margin, expected points, clock, timeouts, Vegas line and the second-half kickoff, with margin and expected points scaled by the square root of time left.
  • Conversion: ridge-penalised logistic regression on third and fourth downs (log distance, field position, goal to go, a fourth-down shift, Vegas line and total, season trend).
  • Field goals: logistic regression with a linear spline in distance (knots at 50 and 58 yards), roof and season trend.
  • Punts and gains: empirical distributions of the next possession by punt spot, and of yards beyond the marker on a conversion.
  • Uncertainty: 200 bootstrap replicates that resample whole games and refit every component. Intervals are 95% unless stated.
  • The 2025 season was held out of every fit and is audited out of sample. nflverse's own win probability is used only as a benchmark.

Limitations

  • No injuries, weather beyond roof type, personnel or play-call quality. A coach may know his kicker is hurt or his line is beaten up.
  • Conversion odds come partly from third downs; fourth-down attempts are a selected sample, so the model may be slightly optimistic in spots where teams rarely go.
  • Each call is scored as if it were a normal play: no fakes, no draw-them-offside, no clock tricks; a fixed amount of clock runs on each branch.
  • The win-probability model is a smooth regression; it is least accurate in the final two minutes of close games, where clock management dominates.
  • Season totals add up probabilities from many plays; they are expected wins, not wins actually lost.
